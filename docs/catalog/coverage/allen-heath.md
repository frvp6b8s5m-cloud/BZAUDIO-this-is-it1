## Important source observations

- The official index describes dLive as a 128-channel / 64-bus platform.
- The Avantis page lists Avantis, Avantis Solo, Avantis Solo Ultra, and Avantis Ultra; it distinguishes 64-channel/42-bus and 96-channel/56-bus configurations.
- The Everything I/O and Audio Networking pages expose named I/O and networking product families that must not be omitted from a complete hardware catalog.
- Category-level names are not product SKUs. No model count or completeness claim is made here.
