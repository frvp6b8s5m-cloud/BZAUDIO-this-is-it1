## Important controls

- Distinguish microphone capsule/model from wireless system package, transmitter/receiver, frequency band, connector, finish, included accessories, and regional package SKU.
- A model name alone is not a complete orderable identity. Capture exact order code/SKU and variant.
- Do not merge wired and wireless variants or different frequency bands.
- Do not treat a featured/best-selling badge as proof that a product is current, available through an authorized distributor, or eligible for dropshipping.
- Images and descriptions require documented usage rights.
- MAP, stock, supplier authorization, warranty, and dropship eligibility require independent evidence.
