# Manufacturer Catalog Source Audit

**Checked:** 2026-11-19  
**Purpose:** Establish official manufacturer sources and a defensible first-pass range inventory. This is a source audit, not a claim that every individual SKU, variant, image, or resale right has been cleared for publication.

## Allen & Heath

**Canonical source:** https://www.allen-heath.com/hardware/

The official hardware index currently exposes these product ranges:

- Digital mixing: dLive, Avantis, SQ / SQ+, Qu, CQ
- Installed audio / processing: AHM, Everything I/O, Audio Networking, GR zone mixers
- Personal monitoring: ME
- DJ: Xone
- Analogue mixing: ZED, MixWizard4
- Broadcast / control: XB, CC Series, IP Controllers
- Accessories and the broader product-type index: accessories, analogue mixers, compact digital mixers, controllers, DJ mixers/controllers, I/O and networking, large digital mixers, personal monitoring, sound management

The official page describes dLive as 128-channel / 64-bus and Avantis as 64–96-channel / 42–56-bus families. Those family-level descriptions are not substitutes for model-specific specifications.

**Coverage status:** Range names recorded. Individual console models, surface/rack variants, I/O options, accessories, lifecycle, and SKU identifiers still require per-product verification before import.

## Yamaha professional audio mixers

**Canonical source:** https://usa.yamaha.com/business/audio/products/mixers/

The current official mixers index lists:

- Digital: RIVAGE PM, DM7, DM5, DM3, MGX, TF, TF-RACK
- Analogue: MGP, MG
- Powered mixers: EMX7 / EMX5, EMX5014C
- Streaming devices: AG Series

The same official index explicitly labels CL Series and QL Series as discontinued. They are excluded from the active catalog and must not be presented as currently marketed products.

**Coverage status:** Series-level index captured. Exact model SKUs, regional variants, product lifecycle, and specification records still require individual product-page checks.

## Shure professional-audio microphones

**Canonical sources:**
- https://www.shure.com/en-US/products/microphones
- https://www.shure.com/en-US/iconic-mics
- https://info.shure.com/en-US/applications/live-performance
- https://service.shure.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/which-microphone-should-i-use-on-selection-guides

Shure's current microphone index shows a 112-result microphone range and includes current listings such as SM7B, SM7dB, SM57, SM58, SM4, and Nexadyne 8. Shure's Iconic Mics page highlights SM58, Super 55, SM7B, SM57, and Nexadyne 8. Its live-performance source also identifies stage and instrument microphone families including KSM11, KSM8, KSM9, Beta 87A / 87C, Beta 58A, SM39, Nexadyne 2 / 5 / 6, Beta 52A, Beta 56A, Beta 57A, Beta 91A, Beta 98AMP/C, Beta 98H/C, SM81, KSM137, and KSM141.

**Coverage status:** Popular/iconic and live-performance model candidates recorded. Exact SKU suffixes, included accessories, wired/wireless distinctions, and product lifecycle need per-variant verification. Do not combine different suffixes into one sellable variant.

## Publication and commercial safeguards

- This audit does not establish BZaudio's authorization to resell or dropship any manufacturer.
- Do not publish MAP until current evidence is matched to the exact variant and permitted for publication.
- Do not publish stock unless a trusted supplier feed provides a current, timestamped match.
- Product imagery must have documented usage rights; manufacturer website images are not automatically cleared for reuse.
- Keep unknown pricing as **Contact for price**. Do not scrape or copy retail prices as BZaudio MAP.
- Until supplier authorization, pricing, fulfillment, and returns terms are documented, all products remain inquiry-only.
