## First verified family discovery

The official mixers page currently exposes:

- Digital: RIVAGE PM, DM7, DM5, DM3, MGX, TF, TF-RACK
- Analogue: MGP, MG
- Powered: EMX7/EMX5, EMX5014C

The same page explicitly labels CL Series and QL Series discontinued. They must not be presented as currently marketed products. Legacy products may be retained in internal historical records only if useful, with lifecycle clearly marked.

### Speakers

The official speakers page exposes category types including powered loudspeakers, powered subwoofers, passive loudspeakers/subwoofers, column line arrays, surface-mount, ceiling, pendant, commercial sound bars, and powered monitor speakers.

Current family examples surfaced include DZR, DXL1K, DXS XLF, DXS mk3, NS-IC, VXH, ESB-1090, HS3/HS4, HS5/HS7/HS8/HS8S, MSP3A, and MS101-4. The same source labels DXS Series and Installation Series discontinued. These examples are not a complete family list.
