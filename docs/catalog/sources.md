# Official Manufacturer Source Register

**Checked:** 2026-11-18  
**Purpose:** Establish authoritative starting points for current physical pro-audio catalog research. These sources do not by themselves establish BZaudio resale authorization, dropship permission, MAP publication rights, or image licensing.

## Allen & Heath
- Official hardware catalog: https://www.allen-heath.com/hardware/
- Official company/product overview: https://www.allen-heath.com/about/
- Official installed-sound product overview: https://www.allen-heath.com/discovery/installed-sound/

The official hardware index lists product families and categories including accessories, analogue mixers, compact and large digital mixers, controllers, DJ mixers/controllers, I/O and networking, personal monitoring, and sound management. It also links current product families such as dLive, Avantis, SQ/SQ+, Qu, CQ, AHM, Everything I/O, Audio Networking, Xone, ZED, ME, GR, MixWizard, XB, CC, and IP controllers.

**Verification rule:** Check each model's official detail page and lifecycle status. A family page or product overview is not proof that every historic model remains active.

**Verification rule:** Keep the catalog scoped to professional audio. Exclude unrelated Yamaha instruments, home audio, consumer headphones, and non-pro-audio products unless a product is explicitly part of the approved professional-audio range.

## Evidence and publication policy
For each product and variant, record:
- Canonical official product URL and checked date.
- Exact model/SKU/MPN and variant identity.
- Current/discontinued status as shown by an authoritative source.
- Verified specifications and source for each important fact.
- Product image source and explicit reseller/web usage rights.
- MAP evidence only when exact-variant match, current effective period, and publication permission are documented.
- Supplier authorization and dropship eligibility separately from manufacturer data.

Do not copy a manufacturer's retail price as BZaudio MAP. Do not infer stock, dealer authorization, warranty, returns, or shipping terms from public product pages.
