# BZaudio Staged Roadmap

## How to read this roadmap

This is a strategic sequence, not a promise of fixed launch dates. Stages are planning horizons and should move forward only when their readiness criteria are met. The company should build a reliable foundation before expanding into complex products and services.

**Guiding rule: deliver a small number of excellent, useful experiences before adding more.**

## Stage 1 — Company and customer foundation

**Planning horizon:** First 6–12 months.

### Focus
Establish a credible BZaudio brand and a clear, easy-to-use public website.

### Outcomes
- Architectural, cinematic, premium visual identity using genuine licensed photography.
- Clear company story, product and solution discovery, and quote/contact journeys.
- Verified product information and a maintainable content/catalog standard.
- Configurable contact destination; quote requests first rather than an assumed checkout system.
- Clear support/contact expectations and privacy/accessibility foundations.
- Initial internal process for recording customer projects and equipment information.

### Readiness gate
Visitors can understand the company, find relevant information, and request help or a quote without confusion. The site is responsive, accessible, performant, and its claims and assets are verified.

## Stage 2 — Customer records and BZaudio for Life foundation

**Planning horizon:** Approximately months 6–18; may overlap with Stage 1 only where dependencies allow.

### Focus
Create a dependable foundation for long-term customer relationships.

### Outcomes
- Secure customer and project records, if and when an appropriate architecture is approved.
- Equipment inventories and model identifiers.
- Document storage and version/source tracking.
- Basic system diagrams and project history.
- Support and service request workflows.
- Clear customer privacy, access, export, retention, and deletion policies.

### Readiness gate
Customer information is protected, accurate, useful, and portable. Authentication and storage are designed and tested before sensitive records are collected.

## Stage 3 — Independent BZaudio Technology platform

**Planning horizon:** Approximately years 1–2.

### Focus
Build the independent software platform around trustworthy system documentation and supported workflows. This is not an operating system.

### Outcomes
- BZaudio System DNA: equipment, configuration, documentation, connection, and service records.
- Model-by-model compatibility knowledge base.
- Signal-flow and system relationship visualization.
- Configuration and firmware history.
- First equipment integrations only where documentation, access, and test equipment permit.
- Prioritize selected Allen & Heath models for initial compatibility research and testing.

### Readiness gate
Each published compatibility claim has a documented test basis, named models/versions, known limitations, and a repeatable verification process. Unsupported models are clearly marked unsupported or unverified.

## Stage 4 — BZaudio Experience Lab

**Planning horizon:** Approximately years 2–3.

### Focus
Make professional audio easier to understand through memorable, high-quality experiences.

### Outcomes
- Interactive product and system demonstrations.
- Listening and educational experiences using owned or licensed media.
- Visual explanations of signal flow, system components, and trade-offs.
- Venue and application learning experiences, clearly distinguishing education from engineered designs.
- Potential physical demonstration events or spaces when commercially justified.

### Readiness gate
Experiences are accessible, useful, accurate about their limitations, and supported by appropriate media rights and technical validation.

## Stage 5 — BZaudio for Life services

**Planning horizon:** Approximately years 3–4.

### Focus
Turn the system record into a useful long-term customer service relationship.

### Outcomes
- Maintenance and service coordination programs.
- Upgrade planning based on documented customer systems.
- Training and education pathways.
- Supported monitoring only for equipment and integrations that have passed verification.
- Qualified service partners where needed.

### Readiness gate
Service promises, staffing, partner responsibilities, escalation paths, data handling, and support capacity are defined before programs are marketed.

## Stage 6 — Industry expansion

**Planning horizon:** Approximately years 4–5 and beyond.

### Focus
Expand only where BZaudio has proven quality, operational capacity, and customer demand.

### Outcomes
- Broader manufacturer integrations and compatibility coverage.
- Strategic partnerships and larger system projects.
- Training and certification programs where authorized.
- Expanded sales and service reach.
- Advanced system planning and acoustic tools based on validated methods and qualified review.

### Readiness gate
Expansion is supported by documented demand, reliable operations, qualified expertise, and a sustainable support model.

## Cross-stage quality requirements

These apply to every stage:

- Keep the customer experience simple, clear, and progressively discoverable.
- Do not overload the website or platform with simultaneous feature launches.
- Prioritize reliability, accessibility, privacy, and performance over feature count.
- Verify product facts, compatibility, calculations, and integrations before making claims.
- Use genuine licensed media and track provenance.
- Keep customer data secure and portable.
- Test on real devices and supported equipment; document limitations.
- Review customer feedback and operational capacity before moving to the next stage.

## Priority rule

When deciding what to build next, prefer work that improves customer trust, clarity, reliability, or a complete customer task. Defer work that adds complexity without a proven customer benefit.

## Roadmap governance

This roadmap is strategic. Each substantial capability requires its own approved specification, technical feasibility review, implementation plan, acceptance criteria, and verification. No implementation begins solely because a capability appears on this roadmap.
