01 / THE IDEAWHY IT EXISTS

MORE THAN AN ACCOUNT

A place in the story. A part in what comes next.

PROJECT ORIGIN is intended for people who choose to support the development of BZaudio. It will recognize early contributors and provide a dedicated space for approved members as the project grows.

CUSTOMER ACCOUNTS ARE SEPARATE. A regular BZaudio account does not automatically create PROJECT ORIGIN membership.

02 / FOUNDER RECOGNITIONMADE TO BE KEPT

A PERMANENT MARK

Your name.
Your number.

Approved Founders are planned to be recognized on the BZaudio Contribution Page and receive an individually numbered black metal Founder’s Edition card, engraved with their name.

Founder numbers will be designed and assigned personally, based on contribution, only after review and approval. Numbers will be unique and permanent.
