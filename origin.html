PRIVATE BRIEFING / 001PROJECT: ORIGIN / CONCEPT OVERVIEW

One connected
world for live audio.

ORIGIN is BZaudio’s long-term vision for bringing the tools audio professionals rely on into one connected ecosystem — not another isolated piece of software, but a platform where the system, the computer, and the physical controls are designed to work together.

01 / THE CORE

A dedicated audio operating system

A purpose-built operating environment designed around real audio workflows. The ambition is to make complex setups feel clear, fast, dependable, and consistent — from preparation through the show itself.

02 / THE DESKTOP

Windows and macOS, connected

A companion software platform for planning, configuring, managing, and moving supported audio work between compatible systems. Desktop tools and the dedicated environment are intended to share one coherent experience.

03 / THE CONTROL

A touchscreen built for the work

A dedicated touchscreen controller designed as a natural physical companion to the software — giving operators a focused, tactile way to interact with the wider platform.

04 / THE ECOSYSTEM

Everything working as one

The larger idea is continuity: one place to prepare, control, organize, and carry audio workflows across the tools people already use, with less friction and fewer disconnected steps.

Compatibility is earned, not assumed. Allen & Heath consoles are the primary initial compatibility target. Support will be verified model by model, with real testing before any compatibility is claimed. Broader interoperability is a goal, not a promise of universal support.
